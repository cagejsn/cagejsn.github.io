Secret Santa

If you want to do the Secret Santa, you can sign up on this page. Further down there’s a form where you can say what you’re into, and mention a few things you’d be happy to get. I’ll read what everyone writes and match people from that.

You can sign up on your own, or as a couple if you’d rather do one gift together. Kids can sign up too, and I’ll match a kid with another kid. Plan on spending somewhere between $80 and $100, and bring the gift with you to family Christmas.

It helps if you’re a little specific. If you just say you like everything, the person who has you won’t know where to start. A kind of coffee, a book, or a link is plenty.

What you write stays with me, and with the person who has you, so they have something to go on.
